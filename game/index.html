1. Cook: tap a station button to start cooking a dish. Watch the timer — food burns if left too long!

4. Combo: fast correct serves build your combo — up to x5 score. Wrong dish or a walk-away resets it.
Goal: serve as many customers as you can in 90 seconds across 3 levels. It gets faster!
